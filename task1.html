About Our Project

Our community service project aims to help people and improve our society through social activities.

Project Objectives

  • Keep our surroundings clean.
  • Create awareness about education.
  • Plant trees and protect nature.
  • Help poor and needy people.

Beneficiaries

Students, children, poor families, elderly people and local communities.

Implementation Stages

  1. Planning the project.
  2. Collecting necessary materials.
  3. Conducting community activities.
  4. Monitoring the activities.
  5. Preparing the final report.
